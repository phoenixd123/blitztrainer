# Blitz Trainer

**Blitz Trainer** runs the Blitzkrieg practice method for you: it builds the stages from a level's start positions, tracks every attempt, and moves your spawn to the next unfinished range automatically.

## Quick start

1. Open a **startpos copy** of the level you're learning (12-18 start positions works best).
2. Pause and press the pink **Blitz Trainer** button, then **Start (Classic)** or **Start (Lite)**.
3. Play. The HUD shows your stage, target range and passes. When a range is done, your next respawn moves on by itself.

## What's different

- **Tracks start positions, not percentages.** Runs are matched to where you actually spawned and how far you got, and every percentage shown matches the game's own 2.1 or 2.2 display.
- **Auto-move spawn.** No startpos switcher needed: finish a range and the next respawn puts you at the next unfinished one. `Q`/`E` cycle start positions, `T` jumps to your next target.
- **Consistency mode.** Require 1-10 passes per range, optionally in a row.
- **Two stage styles.** *Classic* (Rudder's original windows of 1, 2, 3 ... sections) or *Lite* (1, 2, 4, 8 ... half-overlapping windows, for long levels).
- **Long runs count.** A run that covers several unfinished ranges credits all of them (toggleable).
- **Death heatmap** with your worst spots and the ranges with the lowest pass rate.
- **Changed start positions?** Rebuild the profile and keep stats for every range that didn't move.
- **Run validation.** Noclip and speedhack attempts are ignored; lag spikes aren't flagged.
- **Undo last run**, **export/import** via clipboard, per-range stats (passes, streak, attempts, best progress, time, attempts to first pass) and session stats.

Platformer levels aren't supported. The Blitzkrieg method was created by **Rudder**.
